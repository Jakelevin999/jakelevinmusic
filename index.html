01 / ABOUT

Sound, culture
& discovery.

Jake Levin is a Los Angeles–based music producer, DJ, and A&R scout focused on discovering talent and building records at the intersection of culture and sound.

He began DJing at a young age and developed into a producer working across R&B, hip-hop, and electronic music. Alongside his creative work, Jake has experience in A&R, artist discovery, live events, marketing, and music-industry operations.

He currently studies Music Industry at the USC Thornton School of Music and works in A&R scouting with APG.
