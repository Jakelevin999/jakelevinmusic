PROFILE

EAR BEFORE
THE ALGORITHM.

Jake Levin is a Los Angeles based producer and A&R creative with an instinct for records, artists and moments that feel early.

Working across hip hop, R&B and electronic music, Jake approaches production and A&R from the same place: taste first. His work lives between the studio, artist discovery, creative direction and the rooms where music becomes culture.

He currently works in A&R scouting with APG while studying Music Industry at USC Thornton.
